About

Before moving into technology I spent thirteen years working on superyachts in the Mediterranean, finishing as Bosun with responsibility for the deck crew. It is demanding client-service work — the client is on board, the standard expected is high, and problems get solved in front of the person they affect. That is the part of my background that transfers most directly to working with customers and stakeholders.

I came back to Australia and worked on e-commerce, alongside a steady stream of ideas for apps and websites I didn't have the programming ability to build. When LLMs became capable enough to genuinely help with coding, I started turning those ideas into working software, and that is where I first hit the infrastructure layer underneath them.

Infrastructure turned out to be the part I actually wanted. It is where an app idea stops being a sketch and becomes something that runs, scales, costs money, and fails in specific ways. The projects on this site are how I have been learning it. Each one is designed, built, deployed and paid for by me, and written up with the decisions and tradeoffs made explicit — including the ones I got wrong.

I don't have commercial technology experience yet. What I have is AWS certification, a set of systems I built end to end and ran in production, and ten years of working with clients. I am looking for the role where those combine.
